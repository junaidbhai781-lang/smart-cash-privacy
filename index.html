Last updated: 9 October 2026
Privacy PolicyTerms & Conditions

This policy explains what information the Smart Cash Android app (package com.smartcash.user) collects, why, and the choices you have. By using the app you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, phone number and password. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is not visible to us.
  • Optional profile photo if you choose to add one.
  • Task activity: tasks you open or submit, the proof you provide (such as a screenshot, username, link or text), and review results.
  • Wallet data: your balance, rewards, referral code and referral activity, daily check-ins, and transaction history.
  • Payout details: the UPI ID or bank details you add so we can pay your withdrawals.
  • Support messages you send us.

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account and show your wallet and history.
  • To review task submissions and pay approved rewards and withdrawals.
  • To prevent fraud, false submissions and duplicate accounts.
  • To answer support requests and send in-app notifications.

3. Where it is stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database). Some settings and session data are also saved on your device so the app works smoothly. Firebase is run by Google and has its own privacy policy.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the app (such as Google Firebase), or when the law requires it. Tasks can open other apps or websites; those are run by other companies and follow their own privacy policies.

5. App permissions

The app needs internet access. It does not ask for your contacts, SMS or location. If you choose to upload a photo or screenshot, you pick it yourself using your phone's file picker.

7. Security

8. Children
