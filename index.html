Privacy Policy

Smart Cash ("the app", "we", "us") is a task-and-reward app. You complete tasks, submit proof, and receive rewards after review. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: full name, email address, phone number and password. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we do not see or store your password in readable form.
  • Profile: profile photo (DP) if you upload one, and your referral code.
  • Task activity: tasks you open, the proof you submit (screenshot, username or link), and the review result.
  • Wallet and payout details: your balance, earnings, transaction history, and the payout details you add for withdrawals (UPI ID, or bank account holder name, bank name, account number and IFSC).
  • Referrals: the referral code you used and the progress of friends you referred.
  • Support: messages, feedback and reports you send us.
  • Usage status: whether you are online and your last-seen time.
  • On your device: app settings and a copy of your account state are stored on your device so the app works smoothly.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To show tasks, review your proof, credit rewards and process withdrawals.
  • To run referrals, daily check-in rewards and the leaderboard.
  • To answer support requests, send in-app notifications and announcements.
  • To detect fraud, duplicate or fake submissions, and to keep the app safe.

3. What others can see

On the leaderboard, other users may see your display name (shortened), profile photo and reward totals. Your email, phone number and payout details are not shown to other users. Our admin team can see your account details, submissions and withdrawal requests in order to review tasks and pay rewards.

4. Services we use

The app is built on Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database), which stores and processes your data on Google's servers. Google's own privacy policy applies to that infrastructure: policies.google.com/privacy. Tasks may open links to third-party apps or websites; their policies are separate and we do not control them.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to run the app (such as Firebase), when required by law, or to protect the app and its users from fraud or abuse.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed for payouts, fraud prevention and legal reasons. You can ask us to delete your account and data at any time by emailing us from your registered email (see Contact). We will act on the request within a reasonable time, except for records we must keep by law or to settle pending withdrawals.

7. Your choices

  • You can view and update your profile in the app.
  • You can request correction or deletion of your data through Contact.
  • You can log out at any time.

8. Security

We use Firebase security features and reasonable safeguards. No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, so please use a strong, unique password and never share it or your payout details with anyone.

9. Children

Smart Cash is meant for users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has registered, contact us and we will remove the account.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
